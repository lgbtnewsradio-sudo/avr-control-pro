Privacy policy

AVR Control Pro, published by Black Dog Developers, LLC · last updated 21 August 2026

The short version

AVR Control Pro does not collect, transmit, store, or sell any personal information. There are no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry, no advertising, and no third-party services of any kind.

What the app talks to

The app communicates with one thing: the audio/video receiver on your own local network. That traffic never leaves your network. Specifically, it:

  • sends a discovery broadcast on your local subnet to find the receiver;
  • opens a control connection to the receiver to send commands and read its status;
  • requests the current cover art image from the receiver's own built-in web server.

The app makes no connections to the internet. Track titles, artist names and cover art are reported by the receiver itself — the app never contacts Spotify or any streaming service, and you never sign in to anything.

What is stored on your PC

A small settings file holds the receiver's IP address, its port, whether discovery is automatic or manual, and whether the mini window stays on top. It lives in your Windows user profile and is never sent anywhere. Uninstalling the app or deleting that file removes it completely.

Children

The app is a hardware remote control. It is not directed at children and collects no information from anyone, regardless of age.

Changes
